[Quivr](https://github.com/The-Vibe-Company/Quivr) is today's new entry, ranked #10 in the Chatbot category. Its positioning is direct: "Opiniated RAG for integrating GenAI in your apps 🧠 Focus on your product rather than the RAG" — while supporting any LLM (GPT-4, Groq, Llama) and any vectorstore (PGVector, Faiss). What matters here: "opiniated" is a claim to ship working architectural defaults out of the box (chunking, indexing, retrieval plumbing) so a developer isn't assembling a RAG pipeline from a dozen libraries by hand. That trade-off usually comes with the same cost — less flexibility wherever your specific case doesn't fit the built-in defaults. "Any LLM, any vectorstore" sounds universal, but real decoupling from a specific provider only gets proven in practice, not in a README description.
